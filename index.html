09 / ETHICS

Rules of Engagement

What “ethical” means to me. These rules apply to every lab, every tool and every line of code on this site.

01

Permission first

I only test systems I own or have explicit written authorisation to test.

02

Stay in scope

Authorisation has boundaries. I respect the scope, the time window and the rules of every platform.

03

Do no harm

No data destruction, no service disruption, no leaving anything behind that makes a system less safe.

04

Disclose responsibly

Report findings privately, give owners time to fix them, and never publish live exploits.

05

Protect privacy

Other people’s data is not a trophy. I minimise access and never keep what I don’t need.

06

Know the law

Laws differ by country. I learn the rules where I live and where my targets are, before I test.
